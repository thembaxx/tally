## Product context

Tally is for South African learners. Use South African English (`en-ZA`), rand
(`ZAR`, `R`) and South African Standard Time (`Africa/Johannesburg`). Use the
formatting, number-input and calendar helpers in `lib/south-africa.ts`.

Use local accounting terms such as cost of sales, trade receivables/debtors and
trade payables/creditors. Teach statement of profit or loss/income statement and
statement of financial position/balance sheet as equivalent names. Practice
calculations leave VAT out unless a question explicitly includes it; consult
current SARS guidance before adding tax calculations. Preserve saved progress,
lesson IDs, question plans and learners’ own written notes when localising copy.
