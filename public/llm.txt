# Tally
> A patient, interactive accounting tutor for curious minds. Small lessons, 3D models, daily challenges, and gentle review.

## Learning
- [Home](/): A calm dashboard with daily goals and learning progress.
- [Learn](/?tab=learn): Eleven self-paced lessons: The Equation, Debit & Credit, Statements, Timing Matters (accruals), Inventory Stories (FIFO), A Little Over Time (depreciation), Read Between the Numbers (ratios), Plan, Then Compare (budgeting), The Break-even Point, Cash Now, Revenue Later (deferrals), and One Group, One Story (consolidation basics).
- [Practice](/?tab=practice): Date-based daily tasks and spaced review after 1 or 3 days.
- [Me](/?tab=me): Local progress, streaks, and achievement badges.

## Interactions
- Sort assets, liabilities, and equity with swipes or tap buttons. A 3D balance scale illustrates Assets = Liabilities + Equity.
- Enter account names and numerical answers. Recognized synonyms and numeric formatting are accepted.
- Rearrange statement lines with drag handles or keyboard-accessible arrow buttons.
- Rotate and pinch-zoom the 3D scenes. A fallback is available without WebGL.
- Focus mode provides a five-minute timer that pauses when the page is hidden.

## Principles
Uses concrete examples, worked examples, retrieval practice, immediate explanatory feedback, interleaving, self-explanation, and spaced review. Authored lessons and feedback; no generative AI tutor or formal mastery certification. Cash Flow is a clearly labeled upcoming lesson.

## Privacy and platform
No account required. Progress remains in browser local storage and can be exported or reset. No analytics or third-party runtime requests. Installable PWA with offline support after an initial online visit. Supports system, light and dark themes and reduced motion.
