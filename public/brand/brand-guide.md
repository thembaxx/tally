# Tally brand guide

## The idea

**Make accounting click.** Tally makes complicated ideas feel approachable through small discoveries. It is a patient mentor: curious, encouraging and useful to children and adults alike.

The layered lowercase **t** is a small connection made visible. Two warm ribbons meet a cobalt stem, suggesting two sides of a ledger, balance and a lightbulb moment. Rounded ends and restrained depth echo the friendly, colourful app-icon references without reproducing another company's identity.

## Name and voice

- Product name in writing: **Tally**. The visual wordmark reads **tally.**
- Primary tagline: **Make accounting click.**
- Supporting line: **Small lessons. Big lightbulb moments.**
- Explain one idea at a time. Use everyday examples and precise accounting language.
- Use South African English, rand (R/ZAR), and familiar local business contexts. Say “favourable”, “recognise” and “cost of sales”.
- Introduce debtors/creditors alongside trade receivables/payables, and use SAST for learning dates. Practice calculations leave VAT out unless stated.
- Invite curiosity. Celebrate effort and understanding, not speed or perfection.
- Say “Let's think it through” after a mistake. Explain why before asking again.
- Avoid baby talk, pressure, invented credentials, and promises of instant mastery.
- Describe Tally as an accounting learning app, not bookkeeping software or an AI chatbot.

Use cobalt for actions and navigation. Colour supports learning categories; text labels and feedback always carry meaning too. Use ink over pale lesson colours. Apricot and lemon are accents, not small body text on paper. Keep the main reading area quiet and spacious.

The logo's blue, gold, apricot, coral and violet are an illustration palette. Do not sample its gradients for UI text or redefine the accessible interface tokens from them.

## Typography

- **Outfit**: wordmark, headings and expressive short phrases.
- **DM Sans**: body copy, controls and explanations.
- **DM Mono**: XP, amounts, streaks, times and numerical comparisons.
- Local font files and their licenses are included in `/fonts/`.
- The wordmark uses Outfit at weight 600 with slightly tight tracking. Keep the period cobalt.

## Logo usage

- Master transparent mark: `/brand/logo-1024.png`.
- Web mark: `/brand-mark.webp`; transparent, square and lightweight.
- Ready-made app assets: `/icons/icon-192.png`, `/icons/icon-512.png`, `/icons/apple-touch-icon.png`, `/icons/maskable-512.png`.
- Social card: `/og.png` at 1200 × 630.
- Download bundle: `/brand/tally-brand-kit.zip` contains the mark, icons, social card and this guide.
- Use the full-colour mark on paper, white or midnight. Maintain clear space of at least one quarter of the stem's width.
- Minimum standalone mark size: 24 px. At favicon size, use the supplied 32 px asset.
- Scale proportionally. Do not stretch, rotate the saved asset, recolour it, add an outline or put a shadow behind the master mark.
- App tiles use a warm paper background with generous padding; platform masks supply the tile shape. The dedicated maskable asset keeps the symbol inside the central safe area.
- In UI, pair a decorative mark with the readable Tally wordmark. A mark-only link needs an accessible name such as “Tally home”.

## Shape, layout and motion

Rounded cards, soft corners, clear hierarchy and breathing room make space for concentration. Reserve dimensional treatment for objects that help explain an idea. Use short spring transitions for taps and navigation; entrances should guide attention, not delay a task. Respect reduced motion and provide tap and keyboard alternatives to gestures.
